Sheet A1.0

Exam Format Change

SAP replaced the legacy closed-book multiple-choice exam for this certification with an open-book Scenario-Based Assessment (SBA), rolled out through 2025–2026. Old MCQ dumps no longer map to the actual test.

Rev B correction — duration is not a reliable ~2 hrs across the board. It varies by exam, and some SBAs run as a live ~20–30 minute AI chat interview. Confirm P_SAPEA's current figure on its own SAP Learning page before you build pacing drills around any number here.

Grading criterion shifted from "selected the correct option" to quality and completeness of your architectural reasoning.

AttributeLegacy formatCurrent format (2026)
Style80 closed-book MCQsScenario-based, open-book
Duration~180 minVaries by exam — confirm on SAP Learning
InteractionAnswer sheetLive AI-guided interview (Yoodli-based)
Resources allowedNoneSAP Help, Learning, Community, Joule
Result timingImmediateImmediate (AI) or ≤20 days (human review)

Exam-day mechanic: delivered as a live AI-guided virtual interview via your SAP Universal ID, embedded in SAP Learning — no separate install. Corporate Wi-Fi can block the domains it needs, so test your actual exam-day network beforehand or ask IT to allow it. A free account at yoodli.ai will get you used to talking to an AI interviewer beforehand — format only, not real exam content.

Implication: practice extracting constraints from a messy case fast, structuring answers with the framework's own artifact names, and defending trade-offs — not memorizing answer keys.

Architecture Vision & Roadmap

Weight in exam21–30%

Sheet A2.2 — Skill Area 3

Business Architecture

Weight in exam21–30%
  • Recommend a business capability map
  • Model end-to-end business processes

Capability = what the business does, independent of how. Process = how work actually flows. Classic exam trap: capabilities built or duplicated in isolation across business units, so pieces don't reconcile (value proposition vs. cost structure vs. revenue streams).

Sheet A3.0

The "Wanderlust" Case Pattern

SAP's published sample case (a German auto manufacturer moving into EVs) is the template for what the SBA will hand you. Expect a similarly shaped scenario every time: business constraints, a new revenue initiative, a fragmented ECC landscape, and visible EA maturity gaps.

Working order

  • Extract business constraints and strategic priorities from the narrative
  • Extract IT constraints and current-state landscape
  • Identify EA maturity gaps — e.g. no stakeholder map, no link between IT initiatives and strategy
  • Propose a prioritized roadmap, naming the artifact used at each phase

Sequencing rule of thumb for low-maturity orgs: stakeholder involvement first, then business–IT alignment, then architecture development — starting with business architecture, not application or technology.

Sheet G1.0

How to Actually Start

Nothing external gates you here — no waitlist, no enforced prerequisite check at registration, no test-center booking. It's on-demand and self-scheduled, taken from your own workspace once you're ready.

Register without the subscription

You need an SAP Universal ID and an SAP Training Shop account, plus a purchased exam attempt — a single try (CER001) or a six-attempt bundle (CER006). A full SAP Learning Hub subscription is not required to sit this exam.

PathRough costWhat you get
Standalone exam attemptLow hundreds USD — confirm current price on Training ShopJust the attempt(s). No practice systems, no bundled Stay-Certified access
Learning Hub subscription~$1,488 USD/yr, standard edition4 exam attempts, hands-on practice systems, streamlined annual renewal

The one long-term catch: many SAP certifications now renew annually through a "Stay Certified" assessment, and that assessment has been reported to require an active Learning Hub subscription — without one, some candidates report needing to retake the full exam to stay current.

Validity terms are set per certification, not one blanket rule. None of this blocks starting or passing now — just confirm P_SAPEA's specific validity terms on its own certification page before treating "never subscribe" as a permanent stance.

What's actually free

  • The "Becoming an SAP Enterprise Architect" Learning Journey (IEA10) at learning.sap.com — the core free training path
  • A 1-hour "Quick-Start" orientation session for self-directed learners, announced on SAP Community's Learning blog in August 2026 — check there for current sign-up
  • SAP Community's Enterprise Architecture blog space, for first-hand accounts from people who've recently sat it

openSAP, SAP's older free MOOC platform, shut down in 2024 — skip any guide still pointing you there.

Skip the dump sites

Several vendors now sell "AI Role-Play" question banks and flashcards for this exam. The live format is a generative scenario, not a fixed question pool — a memorized answer key structurally can't match what you'll actually see. Save the money.

Recommended sequence

  • Work the free IEA10 Learning Journey and the Quick-Start orientation — this sheet set is a companion to that, not a replacement
  • Check off each topic on the Progress Tracker (next sheet) as you go
  • Repeat the Live Defense Drill (Sheet Q1.0) until you're consistently landing the model reasoning and finishing inside the clock
  • Purchase a standalone exam attempt through SAP Training Shop and self-schedule
